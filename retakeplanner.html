Notes

  • Ranges are the middle 50% of enrolled first-years who submitted scores, from each college's most recent Common Data Set (mostly the fall 2025 class). At test-optional colleges only submitters are counted, which pushes ranges up.
  • Superscores follow each college's stated policy. Where a college only partly superscores, the planner counts your best single sitting to stay conservative.
  • ACT composites since September 2025 average English, math and reading; science doesn't count. Scores on different tests are compared with the official 2018 ACT/SAT concordance and are never mixed into one superscore.
